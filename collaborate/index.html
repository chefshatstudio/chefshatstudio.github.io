Collaborate With Us

We're not hiring you. We're not your boss. We're a bunch of people making games together and splitting whatever we earn. No salaries, no investors, no corpo stuff - just people making cool things.

How the Money Works

Everyone who works on a game gets a percentage of what it earns. We agree on the split before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

There's no upfront pay - but there's also no ceiling. If the game pops off, everyone eats. It's pretty simple: the better the game, the more we all make.
