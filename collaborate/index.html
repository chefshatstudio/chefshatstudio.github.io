Collaborate With Us

Chefshat Studio isn't a traditional employer. We're a group of creators who come together to make games and share in whatever we earn. No salaries, no investors, no strings - just shared risk and shared reward.

Profit-Sharing Model

Every contributor earns a percentage of the project's revenue. Shares are agreed upon before work begins, based on the scope of your role and contribution.

This means there's no upfront pay - but it also means there's no ceiling on what you can earn. If the game does well, everyone does well. We believe this model attracts people who genuinely care about quality, because the better the game, the more everyone earns.
