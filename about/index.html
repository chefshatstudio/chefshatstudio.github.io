About Chefshat Studio

We're a small indie studio that believes the best games come from people who truly care about what they're making.

Chefshat Studio started with a simple idea: what if a game studio worked more like a band than a corporation? No bosses, no salaries, no cubicles. Just a group of passionate creators pooling their skills to make something worth playing. We come together project by project, each person bringing their own flavor to the mix.

How We Work

Chefshat Studio operates on a collaborative profit-sharing model. There are no salaries or upfront pay. Instead, every contributor earns a share of the revenue based on their contribution to the project.

Projects are organized into focused teams. When a new game starts, contributors sign on for specific roles - art, code, music, design, writing - and agree on how the pie gets split. Once the game ships and earns revenue, that revenue is distributed according to each person's share.

This means everyone has skin in the game. The better the game does, the more everyone earns. It aligns incentives and keeps the team motivated to make the best thing possible.
