About Chefshat Studio

We're a small indie studio making games we actually want to play.

Chefshat started because we didn't want to work at a "real" studio. No bosses, no investors breathing down our necks, no endless meetings about the roadmap. Just people who like making games, making games together. We work more like a band than a company - come together for a project, everyone brings their thing, and we split whatever we make.

How We Work

Nobody here gets a salary. Instead, everyone who works on a game gets a cut of whatever it makes. We agree on the split before we start, and when the game ships and earns money, it gets divided up.

When a new project kicks off, people sign on for what they're good at - art, code, music, design, writing, whatever - and we figure out a fair split. Simple as that.

Yeah, it means there's no guaranteed paycheck. But it also means everyone actually cares about making the game good, because the better it does, the more everyone takes home.
